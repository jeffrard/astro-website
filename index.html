Common questions

Painting questions, answered

Is the on-site assessment really free?

Yes. We walk through the space with you, look over every surface, talk through colors, and follow up with a detailed written estimate. There's no obligation to book.

What areas do you serve?

We paint homes in River Falls, Hammond, Hudson, Roberts, Baldwin, New Richmond, Somerset, Prescott, Ellsworth, Spring Valley, Woodville, and Glenwood City, Wisconsin. If you're nearby but don't see your town, ask us.

How long does a typical job take?

It depends on the size of the space and how much prep the surfaces need. Your written estimate spells out the timeline before any work starts, so you know what to expect.

What kind of paint do you use?

Premium, low-VOC coatings chosen for each surface and exposure. If you already have a color in mind, bring it. We'll test samples in your actual light before committing.

When is exterior painting season in Wisconsin?

Exterior paint needs dry weather and temperatures within the range on the product label, which here usually means late spring through early fall. Spots fill up, so it pays to book your assessment early.

Do I need to move furniture or prep anything?

We cover floors, protect and move furniture as needed, and put everything back when we're done. Clearing small breakables and wall hangings beforehand helps the job go faster.

How do I get an estimate?

Call or text (651) 341-7483, fill out the request form, or email hello@astropainting.co. We usually reply within one business day.
