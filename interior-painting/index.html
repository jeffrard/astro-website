Common questions

Interior Painting FAQ

Can you paint while we're living in the house?

Yes. We work room by room, keep work areas contained, and clean up at the end of each day so the rest of your home stays usable.

Will the paint smell?

Low-VOC paints have far less odor than older formulas. Some smell during application is normal, and good ventilation clears it quickly.

Which sheen should I use?

As a rule of thumb: flat for ceilings, eggshell or satin for walls, and semi-gloss for trim and doors. We'll recommend what fits each room during your assessment.

Do you help pick colors?

Yes. Color planning is part of the free on-site assessment. Bring your ideas or start from scratch, and we'll test samples on your walls.
