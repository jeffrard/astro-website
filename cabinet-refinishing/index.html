Common questions

Cabinet Refinishing FAQ

Is refinishing cheaper than replacing cabinets?

Usually by a wide margin. If your cabinet boxes are solid and you like the layout, refinishing gives you a new look for a fraction of the cost of new cabinets.

How durable is painted cabinetry?

Durability comes down to prep. Thorough degreasing, sanding, and a bonding primer under quality cabinet-grade paint give a hard, washable finish.

Can you paint over stained wood cabinets?

Yes. With proper cleaning, sanding, and primer, stained cabinets take paint well. It's one of the most common updates we do.

How long will my kitchen be out of commission?

It depends on the number of doors and drawers and the finish you choose. Your written estimate includes the timeline, and we'll plan the work to keep as much of your kitchen usable as possible.
